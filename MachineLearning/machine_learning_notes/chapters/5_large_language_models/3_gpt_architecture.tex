\section{Architecture}
\paragraph{Purpose}
A GPT-style model keeps \tB{only the decoder stack of the Transformer, without the
cross-attention} (there is no encoder to attend to), and is trained for next-token
prediction.

\paragraph{Theory}
Let $\bm{H}^{(0)}\in\mathcal{M}(\mathbb{R})_{n,d_{model}}$ be the token embeddings plus the
positional information of the $n$ input tokens. Each of the $N$ identical blocks applies, in
the pre-LN form:
\begin{center}
    \frB{$\bm{H}\leftarrow\bm{H}+MHA_{causal}\left(LayerNorm(\bm{H})\right),\qquad
    \bm{H}\leftarrow\bm{H}+FFN\left(LayerNorm(\bm{H})\right)$}
\end{center}
After the last block, a final layer normalization and the \emph{unembedding} give the
logits of every position, and the softmax the next-token distribution:
\begin{center}
    $\bm{Z} = LayerNorm\left(\bm{H}^{(N)}\right)\bm{E}^{T}\in\mathcal{M}(\mathbb{R})_{n,V},
    \qquad \mathbb{P}\left(x_{t+1}\,|\,x_{\leq t}\right) = \mathrm{softmax}\left(
    \bm{Z}_{t}\right)$
\end{center}
where $\bm{E}$ is the embedding matrix (weight tying). The hyper-parameters are the number of
layers $N$, the width $d_{model}$, the number of heads $h$ (with $d_{k}=d_{model}/h$), the
inner size of the FFN ($d_{ff}=4\,d_{model}$) and the context length $n_{ctx}$, that is the
maximum number of tokens the model can read.

\section{Number of Parameters}
Per block, with $h\,d_{k}=d_{model}=d$:
\begin{itemize}
    \item attention: $\bm{W}_{Q},\bm{W}_{K},\bm{W}_{V},\bm{W}_{O}$, four matrices of size
        $d\times d$, that is $4d^{2}$;
    \item FFN: $d\times 4d$ and $4d\times d$, that is $8d^{2}$;
    \item the biases and the layer-norm parameters are negligible.
\end{itemize}
\begin{center}
    \frB{$P\approx 12\,N\,d^{2} + V\,d$}
\end{center}
(plus $n_{ctx}\,d$ if the positions are learned). The block terms dominate for large
models.
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{|l|c|c|c|c|}
    \hline
    Model & $N$ & $d_{model}$ & $V$ & $P$ (formula)\\
    \hline
    GPT-2 small & 12 & 768 & $50\,257$ & $\approx 124$M\\
    GPT-3 & 96 & 12\,288 & $50\,257$ & $\approx 175$B\\
    \hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
For GPT-2 small: $12\cdot12\cdot768^{2}\approx85$M for the blocks and $50\,257\cdot768
\approx39$M for the embeddings. The attention scores add \uB{no parameter} but cost
$O(n^{2})$ in time and memory.

\section{Parameters versus Context}
The parameters $P$ are fixed after training, and they are shared by all the positions. The
\tB{context window} $n_{ctx}$ limits how many tokens the model can attend to at once, and
anything outside it is invisible to the model. The cost of attention grows quadratically
with the number of tokens in the context.
